The sixth living doc, docs/risks.md, is the one risk register, covering business, technical, and team risks, ranked, each with an owner, plus every external prerequisite (an approval, an account, a review, recruitment) with who asked for it and when, updated between checkpoints. Write each risk as a fact about the project, without blame; a problem between teammates goes to your Teaching Assistant (TA).
